Computer Science

Computer Science focuses on programming, algorithms, and solving problems using computers. Students study data structures, databases, operating systems, software engineering, and artificial intelligence. Graduates can pursue careers in software development, web and mobile application development, data analysis, and artificial intelligence.

Advantages of Studying Computer Science:

  1. Programming Skills:

    Learn to design and build software, websites, and applications.

  2. Logical Thinking:

    Develop problem-solving skills through algorithms.

  3. Diverse Career Paths:

    Explore software development, artificial intelligence, data analysis, and cybersecurity.

  4. Personal Projects:

    Turn your ideas into applications and digital products.

  5. Strong Technical Foundation:

    * Gain the knowledge needed to learn new programming languages and technologies.
